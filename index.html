For media buyers, creative team & anyone touching ad copy

The One Rule

Sell the Nalena Program.
Never sell the drug.

5 Principles

Everything flows from these. If you know these, you know what's OK.

🏆

1. The Program Gets Credit

Every claim — weight loss, appetite, energy — must be about "the Nalena program." Never attribute results to the medication itself. The program includes medication + clinician oversight + ongoing support. That's what we sell.

🚫

2. No Brand Names. No Drug Names.

Never say Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, semaglutide, or tirzepatide in any ad. Say "prescription weight loss medication" or "the Nalena program."

⚖️

3. No Equivalence

Never say "same ingredient as," "generic," "equivalent to," or "alternative to" any branded product. Never compare our pricing to branded drugs. We are the Nalena program.

🩺

4. Don't Play Doctor

Nalena doesn't approve treatments, recommend medications, or guarantee outcomes. The provider does. Say "your provider will assess" — never "your treatment is approved."

🤔

5. When In Doubt, Leave It Out

If you're not sure a claim is OK, don't run it. Ask Brayden. Ask legal. One bad ad can kill a 7-figure ad account.

Say This, Not That

Quick reference — green is go, red is stop.

✅ Say This

  • The Nalena program
  • Nalena members see 15-20% weight loss
  • Prescription weight loss medication included
  • Doctor-prescribed / Clinician-guided
  • Prepared by licensed pharmacies
  • Real results from real women
  • Shipping included
  • Select an option for your provider to assess
  • Dosage adjustments, as clinically indicated
  • Results may vary
  • Get back to who you used to be

❌ Never Say This

  • Ozempic / Wegovy / Mounjaro / Zepbound
  • Semaglutide / Tirzepatide (in ads)
  • "Same ingredient as…"
  • "Generic Ozempic" or "Generic Mounjaro"
  • "Clinically proven" / "Backed by science"
  • "FDA-approved" (our meds aren't)
  • "Cheaper than [brand]"
  • "Choose your treatment"
  • "Your treatment is approved"
  • "Free dosage increases"
  • "Like magic" / any guarantee

Required Disclaimers

Copy these exactly. Every ad needs the main disclaimer. Others are conditional.

📋 Main Disclaimer — Every Ad

Results may vary. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved. The Nalena program includes prescription medication, if prescribed by a licensed healthcare provider.

Font: Source Sans 3 · Min 8pt static / 14px video · Color: #785375 or #FFFFFF · Opacity: 85%+ · Bottom of ad · 4+ sec in video

💬 Testimonial Disclaimer — When Using Reviews or Transformations

Outcomes vary based on individual factors including lifestyle and adherence to the prescribed program. Testimonials reflect individual experiences and are not guarantees of specific outcomes.

💊 Medication Disclaimer — When Mentioning Medication Access

Medication eligibility and prescriptions are determined solely by licensed medical providers based on clinical appropriateness. Not all individuals will qualify for medication-based treatment.

Pre-Publish Checklist

Run through this before any ad goes live.

No brand drug names (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound)
No generic drug names in ad copy (semaglutide, tirzepatide)
No equivalence claims (same as, generic, alternative to, cheaper than)
No "clinically proven" / "backed by science" / "FDA-approved"
No guarantee language or percentage success rates
No "choose your treatment" / "approved" / "our recommendation"
All claims tied to "the Nalena program"
No vials paired with efficacy claims or stats
No Nalena branding on medication labels
Main disclaimer present and readable
"Results may vary" near any weight loss claims
Actor disclosure if applicable (top, first 5 sec)
Pricing shows ongoing rate if discounted intro
Content rights secured (owned, licensed, or released)
